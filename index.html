HolyBite – Christian Fasting
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

HolyBite – Christian Fasting is provided by Apkerooo (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how information is processed when you use the app. For privacy questions or requests, email apkerooo@gmail.com.

Information we process

  • Account: your account identifier, email address and name. Firebase Authentication processes your sign-in credentials and account recovery or email verification requests.
  • Profile: the birth date, gender, height, weight and optional profile photo you provide. A selected photo is uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage and its download link is saved with your profile.
  • Fasting: the selected plan and duration, start and goal times, actual ending time, status, elapsed duration, and optional notes and mood. These records may reveal information about your religious practices or health-related habits.
  • Bible reading: completed reading dates, books, chapters, translation and streak information.
  • Account metadata: subscription tier and expiry information where present.
  • Support: messages and screenshots you choose to send using your email application. Review any screenshot before sending it.
  • Advertising and usage: Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics may process device or advertising identifiers, IP addresses, app interactions and technical information according to their service policies.

The app does not request precise device location. Avoid including sensitive information about other people in notes, photos or support messages.

How we use information

We use information to provide sign-in and account recovery, maintain your profile, save and restore fasting sessions and reading progress across devices, calculate reading streaks, display community activity, respond to support requests, display advertisements and understand app usage.

Community fasting count

The updated app maintains a minimal active-fast record containing an account identifier, a fasting-record identifier and the scheduled ending time. Signed-in clients can read these records to calculate the community total. These records do not include your name, email, profile details, fasting notes, mood or Bible readings. The total refreshes periodically and may not reflect changes immediately.

Service providers and sharing

Google Firebase provides authentication, database, photo storage and analytics services. Google AdMob provides advertisements. These providers process information required to operate their services. Profile photos are displayed using download links; anyone who obtains such a link may be able to access the photo.

GitHub Pages hosts these public policy pages. Visiting them involves requests to GitHub. We do not publish your account records, fasting history, Bible readings or photos in the policy repository.

We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to investigate abuse, protect users or respond to valid legal requests. Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own.

Retention and your choices

Saved account information, fasting history and Bible readings are retained to support your account and restore your progress until you delete supported records or request account deletion. You can edit supported profile fields and replace or remove your photo. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or cloud records.

You can use your device’s advertising privacy controls. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your information, or object to certain processing. Contact us to make a request or ask about your choices. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Request account and data deletion

Inside HolyBite: open Settings, choose the account deletion option and follow the confirmation or sign-in verification prompts.

Without the app: email apkerooo@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject “HolyBite account deletion”. State that you want your HolyBite account and associated data deleted. If you cannot access that address, contact us for ownership verification. Never send your password.

Deletion covers your authentication account, profile, profile photo, saved fasting history, Bible-reading records, subscription records held with the account and community active-fast marker. If an in-app deletion fails, contact support to complete the request. Deleting an account does not by itself cancel any subscription managed by an app store.

Provider-controlled logs and backups may remain until removed under the provider’s retention policies. Information legally required to be retained may be kept for the required period. We do not specify a fixed retention period for those provider-controlled copies.

Children

HolyBite is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and arrange deletion.

Security

We use Firebase authentication and access controls to protect saved account data. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account credentials secure.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as the app or its data practices change. The updated date appears at the top of this page.
